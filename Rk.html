🏭 Our Process

How We Make Our Products

Every product goes through a careful, hygienic, and love-filled process to ensure the highest quality reaches your home.

🔍 Step by Step

Our Production Process

From farm to your table — completely transparent

1
🌾

Sourcing the Best Organic Ingredients

We begin by carefully selecting organic fruits, vegetables, grains, nuts, and spices from trusted local farmers. Every ingredient is personally inspected to ensure it's fresh, natural, and free from chemicals or pesticides.

Organic FarmsLocal SourcingChemical-FreeFreshness Checked
2
🧹

Thorough Cleaning & Preparation

All raw ingredients undergo multiple rounds of thorough cleaning with purified water. Fruits and vegetables are washed, peeled, and sorted carefully. Grains and seeds are cleaned, sorted, and dried to remove any impurities before processing.

Purified WaterMulti-Stage CleaningHand-sorted
3
🌞

Natural Drying & Sun-Drying

Fruits and vegetables are naturally sun-dried or carefully dehydrated at low temperatures to preserve all their natural nutrients, vitamins, and enzymes. We never use high-heat processing that destroys nutritional value.

Sun-DriedLow TemperatureNutrients Preserved
4
⚙️

Grinding & Blending

The dried ingredients are stone-ground or cold-milled to a fine powder, preserving their natural oils and nutrients. Each product recipe is mixed in precise quantities to ensure a perfect balance of nutrition and taste in every batch.

Stone GroundCold MilledPrecise RecipeNatural Oils Intact
5
🔬

Quality Testing

Every batch is quality-checked before packaging. We test for freshness, aroma, consistency, and taste. Our team ensures that every packet meets RN Foods' high standards. Only perfect batches are approved for packaging.

Batch TestingAroma CheckConsistency CheckTaste Approved
6
📦

Hygienic Packaging & Sealing

Products are packed in food-grade, airtight, moisture-proof pouches in our hygienic packing unit. All staff wear gloves, masks, and hairnets. Packages are sealed and labelled with manufacturing date and best-before information.

Food-Grade PouchesAirtight SealHygienic UnitLabelled Properly
7
🚚

Safe Delivery to Your Home

Each order is carefully packed with bubble wrap and secure outer packaging for safe transit. We ship with trusted courier partners to ensure your products arrive fresh, intact, and on time. Your health is our delivery promise.

Secure PackingTrusted CouriersOn-Time DeliveryFreshness Guaranteed
